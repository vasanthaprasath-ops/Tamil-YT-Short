# 03 — வெங்காயம் வெட்டினா ஏன் கண்ணுல தண்ணி?
Series: உடம்பு #2 | Status: POSTED 2026-11-18 (rendered v1: 42.2 s, -14.3 LUFS, -1.4 dBTP) | Date: 2026-11-16

## Takeaway
Cutting an onion breaks its cells, so two substances that were kept apart mix and make an irritating gas. The gas reaches your eyes, and the tears are your eyes washing it out. A sharp knife, cutting slowly, breaks fewer cells and throws up far fewer droplets; chilling does not help (2025 study).

## Sources
1. Library of Congress, Everyday Mysteries: "Why does chopping an onion make you cry?" — https://www.loc.gov/everyday-mysteries/food-and-nutrition/item/why-does-chopping-an-onion-make-you-cry/ (cut cells → enzyme → syn-propanethial-S-oxide gas → corneal nerves → tears)
2. Imai et al., "An onion enzyme that makes the eyes water", Nature 419, 685 (2002) — the lachrymatory-factor synthase step; summary at https://www.proteinspotlight.org/back_issues/028
3. Medical News Today, https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/318811 — a sharp knife crushes fewer cells.
5. Cornell (Jung, Wu, Hooshanginejad), PNAS 2025: blunt blades threw up to 40× more droplets than sharp ones; fast cutting 4× more than slow; chilled onions did NOT reduce droplets. Reports: https://www.snexplores.org/article/slow-sharp-cuts-onion-crying , https://physicsworld.com/a/the-physics-behind-why-cutting-onions-makes-us-cry/ . This conflicts with the "chill the onion" tip, so the fridge ending was dropped in v4.
4. Self-defense line (beat 10) is hedged ("இருக்கலாம்"); sulfur compounds as a deterrent to animals is the common explanation (Live Science, https://www.livescience.com/32299-why-does-slicing-onions-make-me-cry.html). Cut beat 10 if Vasanth wants only hard facts.

Not used: the popular claim that the gas turns into sulfuric acid in the eye (not supported by the better sources).

## Delivery map v2: one steady tone, three accents (2026-11-18, replaces the map below)

That breaks our own rule 21 (one target emotion per video), and the v4 docs warn that contrasting emotions in one sentence lower accuracy. The picture, the music stop and the effects already carry the drama; the voice only needs one friendly tone with stress on the right words.

- **Base tone, set once at the top:** a friendly older brother explaining something interesting: warm, curious, conversational, quick. v4 carries an opening emotion across the following lines.
- **Only three accents, where the script turns:** the surprise in the hook (எதுவுமே), the rehook dropping a little lower (line 6), the tease on the twist (மொக்கை).
- **Energy stays in a narrow band:** 3–4, never 2 or 5. Variety comes from stressing one word per sentence, not from changing emotion.
- **Target when measured with `pitch_check.py`:** spread ≈ 10–11 semitones (v3 take was 9.6 and sounded flat; the 3-line v4 test with heavy tags was 12.1), peaks on the stress words.
