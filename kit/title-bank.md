`<Tamil-script question> <emoji> <English keyword> | <Series>`, plus `#tamil #shorts` in the description.

**Every fact needs two sources before you script it.** Titles marked ⚠ touch health: name the health authority in the video.

Status values: `idea` → `researched` → `scripted` → `made` → `posted`.
