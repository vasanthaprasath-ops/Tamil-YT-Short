# Honest Answers Can Win Tamil Shorts

A faceless, AI-animated Tamil Shorts channel can win, but not by copying anything. It wins by putting together three things that already work separately and that nobody in Tamil has combined: Zack D. Films' topic logic (your own body and daily life, with the thing happening on screen in second one), the identity pull that dominates Tamil Shorts (our paati, our city, our food), and an honest verdict. Existing Tamil "elders said" content almost always defends tradition, and it gets 15 to 7,300 views. Honest health myth-busting by doctors gets 1M–4.4M. So the demand is real, and nobody is supplying an honest, entertaining version of it on a recurring basis. Across platforms the mechanics agree. YouTube tests each Short on a small seed audience and widens reach on watch-versus-swipe behaviour. Instagram ranks on watch time, likes and sends. Meta now actively demotes duplicated and unoriginal uploads. AI tools are allowed everywhere. What gets channels demonetised is templated sameness, AI "experts" giving health advice, and health claims that contradict authorities. The hard truth is money. Indian Shorts ad revenue is tiny (blog estimates run from roughly $0.01 to ₹30 per 1,000 views). One researcher read on the YouTube blog that from 1 February 2027 new channels will need 20M Shorts views in 90 days to join the Partner Program, which is about 222,000 views a day sustained. That claim still needs verifying. If it holds, the realistic order of income is brand deals and Shopping affiliate first, an English or Hindi audio track second, and Shorts ad revenue last. Everything below ends in an operating plan Vasanth can start tomorrow.

**How to read this report.** Sourced facts carry an inline link. Statements marked **(inference)** are my judgement built on that evidence. Statements marked **(unverified)** come from a single secondary source, from general knowledge not checked in this research, or conflict between sources. Treat them as things to check, not facts. View counts from YouTube searches are the top results YouTube chose to show on 2026-11-04. They are samples, not averages.

## Three mechanics travel across every platform

**Self-relevance plus a visible first second is the transferable core of Zack D. Films.** On 2026-11-04 his channel had **29.6M subscribers and 4.1K videos**. His top 48 Shorts range from 87M to 255M views, and they split roughly **40% the viewer's own body, 20% everyday-object mysteries, 15% survival, 15% weird history and 5% small moral stories** ([Zack D. Films Shorts tab](https://www.youtube.com/@zackdfilms/shorts)). The counterfactual test in that analysis matters more than the percentages. Take away the animation and a strong topic still works. Take away the self-relevance, or the visual of the thing actually happening, and the video collapses. Animation amplifies the effect; the topic and the first-second visual drive it. His style itself is not replicable and should not be copied. He also benefits from survivorship: a large subscriber base gives every upload a multi-million floor, and hits are a small share of 4,100 uploads. Third-party trackers agree on the scale (about 28–29M subscribers and 72–81B views, almost all from Shorts) but not on exact figures ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@zackdfilms/); [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/channel/zackdfilms)).

**The platforms reward the same behaviour, which is why one master cut can serve all three.** YouTube shows a new Short to a seed audience and widens reach based on how that group reacts. Shorts product lead Todd Sherman put it as "the audience is the algorithm", and said there is no reward for posting a set number per day or at a set time ([OutlierKit transcript of Creator Insider Q&A, Aug 2023](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)). That is still the latest staff statement found. Since 31 March 2025 the public view count includes every replay, but **YPP eligibility and revenue still use "engaged views"** ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted)). Studio's **"Viewed vs. swiped away"** metric measures the first-moment decision directly ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217?hl=en&co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts)). The popular "aim for 70–80% viewed" target has no YouTube source. On Instagram, Adam Mosseri named **watch time, likes and sends** as the top three signals ([Social Media Today, Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-2025/738034/)), and said send *rate* matters more than raw sends ([Social Media Today, 2024](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-post-share-rates-key-driver-reach/716540/)). Instagram also says recommendations no longer favour large accounts ([Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/the-latest-with-instagram?)). All three surfaces now accept roughly 3-minute vertical video, but Instagram brand data shows **45–60 second Reels with the highest engagement rate** ([Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)). That supports a 30–60 second master that works everywhere **(inference)**. **X and TikTok are not worth planning around.** The research found no 2024–2026 data on X video formats, and TikTok has been banned in India since 2020 (general knowledge, **unverified** here), so TikTok matters only as format inspiration.

**The psychology research turns each episode into a repeatable build.** The best-replicated sharing driver is **high-arousal emotion (awe, surprise, anxiety) plus practical value**. Low-arousal sadness *suppresses* sharing, according to an analysis of ~7,000 NYT articles ([Berger & Milkman 2012](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/Virality.pdf)). In 2.73M social posts, out-group language doubled shares but drew anger. **In-group language drew "love" reactions** ([Rathje et al. 2021, PNAS](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/8256037)). For a family-forwarded Tamil channel, that means "our paati, our ooru", never "these foolish people". Curiosity peaks when the viewer half-knows the answer ([Kang et al. 2009](https://escholarship.org/content/qt2zd605r7/qt2zd605r7.pdf?t=rttpgp)). Content shown *during* an open curiosity gap is remembered better ([Gruber et al. 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4252494/)). Guessing before the reveal improves recall even when the guess is wrong, as long as the answer follows immediately ([Kornell et al. 2009, via Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/the-pluses-of-getting-it-wrong/); [Psychonomic Bulletin & Review review](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.3758/s13423-020-01810-y.pdf)). The strongest evidence in the whole set is that **captions improve comprehension, attention and memory for everyone**, from a review of 100+ studies ([Gernsbacher 2015](https://successforkidswithhearingloss.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Video-Captions-Benefit-Everyone_2015.pdf)). Most "first 3 seconds" percentages circulating online have no traceable primary source, so they are not used here.

## Tamil Shorts reward identity and spectacle, but honest answers are an empty shelf

**Tamil facts is a crowded, proven category, and its leaders win on spectacle footage, not argument.** The biggest channels include Tamil Facts (3.39M subscribers, with a recent 28-day subscriber *loss*) ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@tamilfacts_yoga/)), Official Facts (2.62M), Tamil Wonders (1.34M, one Short at 15M), SJ Tamil (382K subscribers, yet "பெரிய கப்பல்களை இப்படி தான் நிறுத்துகிறார்" reached **20M**) and Fast Facts தமிழ் (427K, a betel-leaf hack at 1.8M) ([YouTube search "tamil facts shorts"](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=tamil+facts+shorts); [YouTube search "தெரியுமா shorts"](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=தெரியுமா+shorts)). Their house template is a Tamil-script hook, a 😱 or 🤯 emoji, a "| Channel" tag and English hashtags. That is the baseline to match, not a way to stand out. A faceless animated channel cannot beat them on real-world spectacle footage. It has to beat them on story, character and payoff **(inference)**. The long-form anchor, Madan Gowri (9.01M), said Tamil drew an audience because "there were less creators in Tamil" ([TNM](https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/science-history-how-youtuber-madan-gowri-s-tamil-explainers-became-hit-95309)).

**The "is it true?" question has proven demand, and nobody answers it honestly.** The "முன்னோர்கள் சொன்னது அறிவியல்" niche is pro-tradition justification getting **304 to ~7.3K views** ([YouTube search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=முன்னோர்கள்+சொன்னது+அறிவியல்+காரணம்)). A near-identical concept, "பாட்டி சொல்லும் கதை – Episode 2 | உண்மையா? பொய்யா?", got **63 views** ([YouTube search "உண்மையா பொய்யா shorts"](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=உண்மையா+பொய்யா+shorts)). That is the warning: the concept alone does not carry a video. Execution does. Meanwhile astrology "is it true?" Shorts get 176K–437K ([same search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=உண்மையா+பொய்யா+shorts)), uncritical paati-vaithiyam remedies get 50K–250K ([YouTube search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=paati+vaithiyam+science)), and **doctor-led health myth-busting reaches 1.1M–4.4M** ([YouTube search "health myth tamil doctor shorts"](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=health+myth+tamil+doctor+shorts)). Tamil audiences demonstrably accept "this is false" on body and food topics, which makes those the safest places for a POI verdict. Astrology and drishti carry the same backlash risk as the temple topics already dropped **(inference)**.

**City identity is huge, but it lives in comedy and food. Explainers win on "why is X different" questions, not fact lists.** Slang-rivalry comedy reaches 1M–7.7M ("Chennai Girl Vs Nellai Aunty" 7.7M). Food reaches 10M–13M ([YouTube search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=tirunelveli+slang+comedy+shorts); [YouTube search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=madurai+la+mattum+shorts)). The explainer hits are single questions: "Which Is Tamil Nadu's Capital Before Chennai?" **11M**, "Why Pondicherry Is Separate from Tamil Nadu?" **3.9M**, "What If Tamil Nadu Became an Independent Country?" **3.3M**. Generic "Chennai Facts" lists sit around **1.1K** ([YouTube search "chennai facts tamil shorts"](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=chennai+facts+tamil+shorts); [YouTube search "what if tamil shorts"](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=what+if+tamil+shorts)). The literal "Madurai la mattum" phrase is used as a meme, not as an explainer series, so the weekly city slot should be built around one "yen?" question per city.

**Human-body animation is under-supplied in Tamil, and the topic matters more than the channel.** "என்ன உறுப்பு அது?" hit 3.3M. One 3D AI channel's HEART Short got **949K** while its LIVER and BLOOD Shorts got **53K and 47K** ([YouTube search "human body facts tamil shorts"](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=human+body+facts+tamil+shorts)). Survival content in Tamil barely exists: one fire-lighting Short at 2.9M, flood-survival Shorts around 1K. Tamil AI-animated Shorts are common but mostly gag comedy. The standout is a **recurring character series** ("Tamil alien muthupandi", Part 41, **7.9M**), while low-effort clones get 24–200 views ([YouTube search "tamil ai animation shorts"](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=tamil+ai+animation+shorts)). Title language is mixed: Tamil-script hooks with English keywords dominate, Tanglish dominates comedy, and several 3M–11M hits about Tamil Nadu use **English-only** titles.

**The national evidence points the same way.** India's biggest Hindi/English facts channel, FactTechz (~21M), has its top recent Shorts at 9.9M–20M, all built on "this affects you or your country" hooks: Indian law, money and prices, gadgets ([FactTechz Shorts, yt-dlp sample of latest 400](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCGdPm5Aq081vVD7ih9jZf6Q/shorts)). The Infographics Show's top Shorts lean on "what happened to", "what if" and "why X didn't Y" dark curiosity ([The Infographics Show Shorts](https://www.youtube.com/@TheInfographicsShow/shorts)). Localisation is a proven lever. GENIAL, a Spanish Bright Side, reached 32M+ ([AWISEE](https://awisee.com/blog/successful-faceless-youtube-channels/)), and a Hindi dub channel went from 1,670 to 186K subscribers in a month ([vidIQ, Feb 2024](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/10-small-youtube-channels-hit-100k-subs-february)).

## AI is allowed; templates, fake experts and health claims kill channels

**YouTube's line is creative direction, not AI use.** On 15 July 2025 "repetitious content" became **"inauthentic content"**. It is not monetisable when it "looks like it's made with a template", is a slideshow or scrolling text with little narrative or educational value, or is "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production". Explicitly allowed: a repeated intro and outro, series where each video has a distinct concept, and AI used to visualise a character or narrative the creator invented. The policy applies to the **whole channel**, so a run of lazy uploads can demonetise the good ones too ([YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)). A July 2026 restructure added two named categories. "Unsatisfying or off-putting" covers stitched shock AI clips and deceptive realistic fakes. **AI personas presenting themselves as human experts on health, finance, legal or politics** are not monetisable ([Tubefilter, 13 Jul 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/)). The channels YouTube removed or wiped were the template-repetitive, plotless, kid-bait or IP-riding ones. Kapwing counted 278 slop channels with 63B+ views among the world's top-trending channels, including India's Bandar Apna Dost at about 2B views ([Kapwing, Nov 2025](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/)). Counts of terminated channels vary by outlet and are **unverified** ([OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-2026/)).

**Disclosure is narrow, but health rules are strict.** An AI label is required for *realistic* synthetic content: a real person saying something they didn't, altered real events, or a realistic scene that never happened. It is **not required for fully animated or fantastical visuals, for script or caption help, or for cloning your own voice for voiceovers or dubs** ([YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)). The medical misinformation policy prohibits contradicting health-authority guidance on prevention, transmission or treatment. Debunking is protected only when the video clearly disputes the myth with context, such as "countervailing views from local health authorities or medical experts". The first violation brings a warning, a repeat within the window brings a strike, and repeated strikes end in termination ([YouTube Help 13813322](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13813322?hl=en)). Health information panels in India are reserved for licensed professionals ([YouTube Help 12796915](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12796915?hl=en-GB)), so the channel will never carry one. Reading text you did not write, such as a Wikipedia paragraph or a forwarded WhatsApp message, counts as reused content even with permission ([YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)).

**Meta penalises duplicates and engagement bait.** Since April 2024, when Instagram finds identical content it recommends only the original ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html)), and 2026 rules extended this to aggregators ([PetaPixel, Apr 2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)). Since July 2025, Facebook cuts distribution on *everything* an account posts, and removes monetisation for a period, if it repeatedly reuses others' content ([Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/facebook-cracking-down-unoriginal-content-ai-replicas/753001/)). Facebook demotes "tag a friend", "share if you agree" and "comment YES" wording ([TechCrunch, 2017](https://techcrunch.com/2017/12/18/facebook-is-clamping-down-on-posts-that-shamelessly-beg-for-your-engagement/?ncid=rss)). That source is from 2017 and its current status was not reconfirmed. Watermark demotion is widely reported but **unverified** in any official statement ([dot.la](https://dot.la/instagram-tiktok-content-2657200198.html)), so upload clean masters, never a downloaded copy.

**The myth format should follow the debunking evidence, not fear it.** The "backfire effect" is rare. Across 5 experiments, more than 10,000 people and 52 issues, none backfired ([Swire-Thompson et al. 2020](https://www.bu.edu/ballab/pubs/swire-thompson2020.pdf)). The consensus Debunking Handbook prescribes **Fact → Myth (mentioned once, with a warning) → Fallacy (why people believed it) → Fact** ([CSSN](https://cssn.org/the-debunking-handbook-2020-a-consensus-based-handbook-of-recommendations-for-correcting-or-preventing-misinformation/)). The research note did not re-read the PDF text, so check the exact wording. In India, social corrections reduced belief even for identity-anchored beliefs ([Badrinathan & Chauchard 2023, via Scroll](https://scroll.in/article/952227/a-matter-of-fact-new-study-shows-how-indians-respond-to-mis-information-on-whatsapp)). A 2025 replication found only small effects ([Strathprints](https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/93635/)), so expect modest belief change per video. People in Indian WhatsApp groups almost never correct each other ([arXiv 2407.08172](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.08172)). That is the channel's social job: the grandchild forwards the video instead of confronting paati **(inference)**.

## Shorts ad money is thin, and the bar may double on 1 February 2027

**Current rules are confirmed.** The ad-revenue tier of YPP needs **1,000 subscribers plus 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days** (or 4,000 long-form watch hours in 12 months). Review takes about a month ([YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)). Shorts revenue is pooled per country and split by share of engaged views. Creators keep 45%. A Short with no licensed music sends all its share to the creator pool ([YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)).

**The February 2027 change is now confirmed by multiple outlets** (verified 2026-11-04 via [NewscastStudio](https://www.newscaststudio.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-monetization-changes-2027/), [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/), [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/youtube-partner-program-doubles-entry-requirements-2027), [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-cuts-shorts-pay-for-channels-under-10m-views-from-february-2027/)); the official blog post itself was not opened. Coverage agrees that existing partners are exempt from the new entry bar, and that the fan-funding/Shopping tier stays at **500 subscribers + 3,000 watch hours or 3M Shorts views in 90 days**. A YouTube blog post dated 10 August 2026 says that from **1 February 2027 new applicants need 20M Shorts views in 90 days or 8,000 watch hours** (still 1,000 subscribers). It also said **every channel will need 10M Shorts views in the trailing 90 days to earn Shorts revenue in a given month**, that Shopping and fan-funding entry bars are unchanged, and that milestone incentives for channels under 10M are promised with "details soon" ([YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/)). The rule for applications pending on 1 February is unpublished, and outlets differ on whether the 10M Shorts floor applies only to the Shorts creator pool. If it is true, the arithmetic is stark. 20M views in 90 days is **~222,000 views every day**, and staying paid needs ~111,000 a day. A channel launched in November 2026 will almost certainly face the 20M bar **(inference)**. The lower fan-funding/Shopping tier (500 subscribers plus 3,000 watch hours or 3M Shorts views in 90 days) is confirmed by the same coverage. That is the realistic first monetisation gate.

**What the views are worth is uncertain by an order of magnitude.** Blog estimates for Indian Shorts range from **₹5–₹30 per 1,000 views** ([upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/youtube-cpm-india-guide-2026/)) down to **$0.01–$0.03 per 1,000 for regional-language Shorts** ([identitykit.in](https://www.identitykit.in/blog/youtube-shorts-monetization-india-2026)). Another puts 1M Indian Shorts views at ₹900–₹3,800 ([schedpilot](https://schedpilot.com/how-much-youtube-pays-for-1-million-views-in-india/)). On those numbers, 10M Tamil Shorts views pays somewhere between roughly ₹8,000 and ₹3,00,000. The lower half is more plausible for an India-only Tamil audience **(inference)**. Global Shorts RPM is also low (~$0.03–$0.08 per the same schedpilot estimate), so an English dub is mainly a *reach* multiplier, not a rate multiplier **(inference)**.

**The reachable money sits beside the ad pool.** **YouTube Shopping affiliate in India opened to channels with 500 subscribers in March 2026**. It still requires YPP membership, and partners include Flipkart, Myntra and Nykaa. Indian affiliate payouts rose 160%+ year on year ([Google India blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/earn-earlier-expanding-youtube-shopping-to-creators-with-500-subscribers/); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/advertising/youtube-india-creator-payouts-soar-160-percent-with-expanded-retail-partner-ws-lo-111332.htm)). The 500-subscriber Shopping gate appears to sit on the lower YPP tier, which is **unverified**. Nano creators with 1K–10K subscribers reportedly get **₹2,000–₹8,000 per integrated mention** (anecdotal) ([souravjoshivlogs](https://souravjoshivlogs.in/brand-deals-for-youtubers-in-india/)), and Shorts rates run 40–60% below long-form ([influencerfee](https://influencerfee.com/blog/youtube-shorts-creator-rates/)). Meta pays almost nothing directly in India. Facebook content monetisation is invite-only, and its Reels ad programmes ended on 31 August 2025 ([clip-short](https://www.clip-short.com/guides/facebook-content-monetization)). Instagram offers Gifts from 500 followers, but brand deals are the real income there ([Stan](https://stan.store/blog/how-to-monetize-instagram-2026/)). Cross-posting builds audience and brand-deal leverage, not payouts.

**Dubbing is now cheap.** YouTube auto-dubbing opened to all creators on 4 February 2026 ([gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/02/05/youtube-makes-auto-dubbing-available-to-all-creators-worldwide/)). Whether Tamil is a supported source language, and whether Shorts get auto-dubs, is **unconfirmed**. Guides say multi-language audio tracks work on Shorts ([videodubbing.com](https://videodubbing.com/blog/post/youtube-multi-language-audio-tracks-setup-guide-2026/)). Meta AI reel translation with voice dubbing and lip-sync now covers Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali and Marathi on Instagram and Facebook ([about.fb.com](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/11/instagram-empowers-creators-to-go-global-with-local-voice-translations-and-fonts/amp/)). An extra audio track on the *same* Short pools every language's views toward the 10M/20M bar. A separate English channel splits them **(inference)**.

## The operating plan

Each item is tagged **[D]** data-backed (sourced above), **[I]** inference, or **[U]** unverified.

### 1. Channel identity

**Promise [I]:** "Things in a Tamil person's life you never questioned, shown happening and honestly answered." This is broad, like Zack's "Things I Find Interesting", but every topic must pass one test: *does a Tamil viewer have this on their body, in their house, on their street or in their family's memory?* FactTechz's "affects you or your country" hooks and Zack's 40% body share both support this filter [D].

**Look [I]:** One house style that is *not* Zack's realistic-3D look, because copying his look risks being derivative. A practical option is a warm, slightly stylised 3D or "clay" look with a fixed Tamil palette (turmeric yellow, kolam white, temple-red accents), always in vertical close-ups. Lock it with a style reference sheet and reuse the same prompt prefix in every image tool. Fully animated or fantastical visuals need no AI label [D]. Avoid photoreal humans in realistic disaster scenes, or label them [D].

**Recurring characters [I, supported by D]:** Recurring-character series outperform one-off AI gags in Tamil (muthupandi Part 41 at 7.9M [D]). Use two characters, fixed across every video:
- **Kutty / Mani**, an ordinary Tamil person in his 20s with one fixed outfit (for example a mustard half-sleeve shirt). Things happen *to* him: the splinter, the flooded car, the ear pop on the ghat road. He is the "this could be you" body.
- **Paati**, who appears in the Paati series and whenever a household rule comes up. She is wise, warm and never mocked. She is *never* presented as a doctor or expert, because AI health personas are demonetised [D].
- The **verdict stamp** (UNMAI ✅ / PAADHI UNMAI ⚠️ / POI ❌) works as a third "character", with a consistent colour and sound sting. MythBusters' three-tier verdict is the precedent [D].

**Voice [D + I]:** Vasanth's own Tamil voiceover is the narrator, off-screen. It is the strongest defence against "inauthentic" flags, and cloning your own voice later for dubs needs no disclosure [D]. Keep the tone calm and plain, use second person ("unga veetla…"), and use spoken Tamil, not formal written Tamil [I].

**Channel name ideas [I]** (check handle availability on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook first, and avoid "Facts" because it is generic and crowded [D]):

### 5. Quality and policy rules

These are non-negotiable, and each one comes from a sourced rule.

**Inauthentic content.** Every video has a distinct question, a distinct shot list and a real educational payload. Fixed elements (intro sting, verdict stamp, characters) are fine; a fixed *story* with the fact swapped is not. No slideshows of stills with narration. Animate. Since policy applies channel-wide, one lazy batch can cost the whole channel's monetisation ([YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)).

**AI disclosure.** Animated or stylised visuals and your own cloned voice need no label. Any photoreal scene of a real-looking event, place or person needs the altered-content toggle. When in doubt, disclose; disclosure does not reduce reach ([YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)).

**Health misinformation.** Never give a "true" verdict to a remedy that contradicts health-authority guidance. State the correct position early and name the authority on screen or in the description, as the dispute-with-context exception requires ([YouTube Help 13813322](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13813322?hl=en)). Never let Paati or any character pose as a doctor ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/)). Keep body videos *explanatory* ("what is happening inside"), not *prescriptive* ("do this to cure") **(inference)**. End on the fact, never on the myth as the last on-screen text, to avoid continued influence ([CSSN Handbook](https://cssn.org/the-debunking-handbook-2020-a-consensus-based-handbook-of-recommendations-for-correcting-or-preventing-misinformation/)).

**Originality on Meta.** Upload the clean master natively to each platform under accounts you own, YouTube first. Never re-share a downloaded or watermarked copy ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagrams-algorithm-overhaul-will-reward-original-content-and-penalize-aggregators-130018977.html); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/facebook-cracking-down-unoriginal-content-ai-replicas/753001/)).

**Respect rules.** Never mock elders. Frame it as "here's what they noticed, here's what science knows now". Use in-group warmth, never out-group put-downs ([Rathje et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/8256037)). God, temple and ritual "facts" stay out.

**No licensed music.** Licensed music shrinks the creator-pool share ([YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)).

### 6. Posting cadence and the 90-day plan

So the plan replaces the earlier "1 per day for 30 days" with a sustainable ramp **(inference)**. Post on all three platforms the same day, at a consistent time for habit, not for the algorithm.

### 7. Weekly metrics and decision rules

**Read every Sunday (30 minutes):**

| Platform | Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | **Viewed vs. swiped away** per Short [D] | Hook strength. Compare against *your own* median, not blog targets |
| YouTube | **Average % viewed** [D] | Pacing and loop (over 100% means rewatches) |
| YouTube | **Engaged views** (Advanced mode) [D] | The number YPP and revenue actually use |
| YouTube | Subscribers gained per 1,000 engaged views [I] | Whether the *channel* earns a follow, not just the video |
| YouTube | Comments per 1,000 views; share of comments that are genuine guesses or debates [I] | Whether the verdict device is working |
| Instagram | Average watch time, likes per reach, **sends per reach** (calculated by hand) [D] | Mosseri's top signals |
| Facebook | 3-second views, minutes viewed, shares [I] | Secondary; watch for reach drops that signal duplicate penalties |

**Decision rules [I]** (judge a series only after ≥4 episodes):
- **Hook problem:** a Short in the bottom quartile on Viewed% means you rewrite the first frame and line, not the topic. Re-test the same topic with a new opening via Trial Reels.
- **Double down:** a series in the top quartile on both Viewed% and subscribers per 1,000 views gets ≥40% of next month's slots and the better video-credit budget.
- **Kill or rework:** a series whose median engaged views sit under half the channel median after 6 episodes, *and* whose Viewed% is below the channel median, gets dropped. Its best single episode becomes a one-off inside a stronger series.
- **Topic over series:** one Short at 5× the channel median means you make 3 follow-ups on the *same* specific topic within 2 weeks (the HEART-vs-LIVER lesson).
- **Kill switch on risk:** any health-policy warning stops all health-adjacent topics until the script process is fixed.

### 9. Growth levers

**Series and recurring character.** Fixed devices (opening question, Paati, Kutty/Mani, the verdict stamp and sting) make every cross-post recognisable. Recurring-character Tamil AI series outperform one-offs ([YouTube search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=tamil+ai+animation+shorts)). Chain episodes with playlists and the related-video link **(inference)**.

**Comments without bait.** Ask for real information ("Unga paati idhukku enna solluvaanga?", "Unga ooru-la idha enna-nu solluvaanga?") and for the pre-verdict guess. Never use "comment YES / tag a friend" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2017/12/18/facebook-is-clamping-down-on-posts-that-shamelessly-beg-for-your-engagement/?ncid=rss)). Pin the best debate comment, and turn good viewer myths into episodes credited to the commenter **(inference)**.

**WhatsApp share design.** Make the final verdict frame a clean, screenshot-able card carrying the true takeaway in one line. It is the face-saving correction a grandchild forwards to the family group **(inference, built on [arXiv 2407.08172](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.08172))**.

**Collabs.** Pair with small Tamil doctors, science teachers or city creators: their credibility on health topics, your animation **(inference)**. Doctor-led myth content already performs at 1M+ ([YouTube search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=health+myth+tamil+doctor+shorts)).

**Cross-posting.** Publish YouTube first, then native clean uploads to Instagram and Facebook the same day. Use Trial Reels to pre-test hooks ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/meta-rolls-out-india-specific-instagram-reels-tools-and-new-regional-fonts-for-edits-app-84992.htm)).

**Title language tests.** Run some English-titled episodes on Tamil Nadu-wide topics, since several 3M–11M hits used English titles ([YouTube search](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=chennai+facts+tamil+shorts)).

## Conclusion

The research changes the bet. It is no longer "can AI animation make a Tamil Zack D. Films?". The question is whether Vasanth can be the most trustworthy answerer of questions Tamil families already argue about. The animation is the cheap, copyable part. The defensible assets are the honest verdict, the in-group warmth, and his own voice. Those are exactly what YouTube's inauthentic-content rules, Meta's originality rules and the debunking research reward, and exactly what the pro-tradition and spectacle-footage channels lack. The two Tamil results that matter most sit side by side: honest doctor myth-busting at millions of views, and a near-identical paati concept at 63. Together they show the market is open and that execution, not the idea, decides the outcome.

The money evidence points the same way. If the February 2027 bar is confirmed, Shorts ad revenue becomes a lagging reward for a channel that already has audience trust, not a business model for a beginner. So the first 90 days should be judged on learning, not income: Viewed%, subscribers per 1,000 views, and whether people forward the verdict cards. Those numbers decide which two series earn the budget. The February 2027 YPP rule has since been confirmed by multiple outlets. Two unknowns remain, both checkable in a day: whether Tamil works with YouTube's dubbing, and whether CapCut is usable in India.
