# YouTube Shorts: Algorithm, Monetization, AI-Content Rules (state as of Nov 2026, India focus)

Research date: 2026-11-04. Official YouTube pages are cited for rules. Earnings figures from blogs/creators are **anecdotal / unofficial estimates** and are marked as such.

## (a) How does Shorts distribution work, and how are views counted?

### Takeaway
A new Short is shown to a small "seed" audience; reach widens or stops based on how that group reacts (watch vs swipe, completion, rewatch). YouTube staff say there is no posting-count or time-of-day trigger. Since 31 March 2025 the public Shorts view count includes every play and replay, with no minimum watch time. Monetization and YPP still use **engaged views**, the older stricter count.

### Cited Findings
- Todd Sherman (YouTube Shorts product lead), Creator Insider Q&A, 23 Aug 2023: the system tries to "find a set of people that may enjoy your video and depending on how that goes, it may get a lot more traffic". He also said: "The audience is the algorithm." — [OutlierKit transcript summary with timestamped links](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); original video: [Creator Insider](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU)
- In the same Q&A, Rene Ritchie (YouTube Creator Liaison) described early Shorts views as "more like impression than traditional views". Sherman called a new creator's early views "exploratory". — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- Sherman on reasoning: Shorts are judged differently because viewers swipe through a feed rather than click deliberately. — [Shopify blog](https://www.shopify.com/blog/youtube-algorithm) (secondary)
- The Studio metric "Viewed vs. swiped away" shows "the percentage of times that viewers viewed your Shorts versus swiped away". — [YouTube Help, Shorts analytics](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217?hl=en&co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts)
- The often-quoted target of "70%+ viewed" has no YouTube source. It comes from third-party blogs only. — [socialfollowers.uk](https://www.socialfollowers.uk/blogs/youtube-shorts-length/) (unverified)
- Blogs disagree on click-through rate. Some say CTR plays little role in Shorts; others say it matters. Neither side cites YouTube. — [crklr](https://crklr.com/news/how-to-optimise-youtube-shorts-for-seo/) vs [Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/youtube-algorithm)
- **View-count change:** from 31 March 2025, a Shorts view counts each time a Short starts or replays, with no minimum watch time. The old metric was renamed "engaged views" and is still in Analytics (Advanced mode). — [TechCrunch, 26 Mar 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2025/03/26/youtube-shorts-views-counting-stats/); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/)
- YPP eligibility and revenue sharing still use engaged views, not the new public count. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/26/youtube-is-changing-how-youtube-shorts-views-are-counted)
- Sherman (2023) on what a view should mean: "What we try and do with a view is have it encode for your intent of watching that thing." — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/). The March 2025 public-count change moved away from this, but engaged views keep the intent threshold.
- YouTube blog explainer on engaged views: "clicking to watch or watching past the initial seconds". — [blog.youtube](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/engaged-views-youtube-explained/) (date per OutlierKit's source list: 19 Aug 2026)

### Inferences
- Because replays now count as public views, "views" can exceed unique viewers by a lot. Judge performance on engaged views, Viewed vs Swiped Away, and average % viewed, not on the headline number.
- A seed-test system rewards the first 1–2 seconds (the swipe decision) and full-watch or loop behaviour. For a Tamil science-facts Short, the hook frame and the first spoken line carry most of the weight.

### Gaps
- No official 2025–2026 YouTube statement gives Shorts ranking weights or numeric thresholds such as a % viewed target.
- Almost every direct staff quote found dates from August 2023. I found no 2026 Creator Insider or Liaison transcript about Shorts ranking.
- One blog mention (vidIQ) of a further "count from first frame" change on 24 Aug 2026 could not be verified against a primary source.

## (b) What metrics matter in YouTube Studio?

### Takeaway
For Shorts the useful Studio metrics are Viewed vs Swiped Away (the swipe decision), average % viewed (often over 100% because of loops), engaged views (what pays and what counts for YPP), and subscribers gained per Short.

### Cited Findings
- The Shorts analytics tab exposes "Viewed vs swiped away". — [YouTube Help 12942217](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217?hl=en&co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts)
- Engaged views are kept in Analytics (Advanced mode) after the March 2025 change. — [Yahoo Tech via search summary](https://tech.yahoo.com/general/articles/youtube-changing-view-means-youtube-162942286.html)
- Shorts revenue is allocated by "share of total engaged views". — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- YPP's Shorts path counts only "qualified Shorts views" from public Shorts in the Shorts Feed. Private, unlisted and deleted Shorts, ad campaigns and image posts are excluded. — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)

### Inferences
- Track engaged views per Short as the main KPI, because both YPP and revenue use them.
- Use Viewed vs Swiped Away to judge hooks, and average % viewed to judge pacing and loop design.

### Gaps
- YouTube publishes no official benchmark ranges for these metrics.

## (c) Monetization requirements and realistic India/Tamil earnings

### Takeaway
Today a channel joins YPP's ad-revenue tier with 1,000 subscribers plus either 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days or 4,000 long-form watch hours in 12 months. **From 1 Feb 2027**, new applicants need 20M Shorts views in 90 days or 8,000 watch hours. Every channel will also need 10M Shorts views in the trailing 90 days to earn Shorts ad revenue in a given month. Creators keep 45% of their allocated Shorts pool. Indian Shorts RPM estimates are very low, roughly ₹5–₹30 per 1,000 views (unofficial).

### Cited Findings
**Current YPP (as of Nov 2026)**
- Thresholds: "1,000 subscribers with 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months" or "1,000 subscribers with 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days". — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)
- Review takes about a month. After a first rejection: appeal within 21 days or reapply after 30 days. Later rejections mean a 90-day wait. Monetization may be switched off after 6+ months with no uploads or posts. — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)
- Creators must accept updated YPP terms in Studio by 31 Jan 2027 to keep full monetization. — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)

**Feb 2027 changes (announced 10 Aug 2026)**
- New entry bar: 8,000 qualified watch hours in the last 365 days or 20M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days. 1,000 subscribers is unchanged. Creators already in YPP are not affected by the entry change. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/)
- From 1 Feb 2027, Shorts ad and subscription revenue sharing in a month needs 10M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days. Channels below that stay in YPP and keep long-form earnings. Shorts earnings resume automatically when the threshold is crossed again. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/); [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en) (the measurement window ends on the 15th of the preceding month)
- Fan Funding and Shopping entry thresholds are unchanged. YouTube promised milestone incentives for channels under 10M (Shopping bonuses, brand-deal incentives, trend boosts), with "details soon". — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/)
- Premium Lite is expanding to every Premium country. Creators get 45% of Shorts Premium allocation. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/)
- Pending applications on 1 Feb 2027: the transition rule is unclear, and no published rule was found. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-partner-program-changes-2027/) (secondary)

**Shorts revenue-sharing mechanics**
- Ads shown between Shorts in the feed are pooled monthly per country. A Short with no music sends 100% of its associated revenue to the Creator Pool; one track sends half, two tracks a third (the rest pays music licensing). Creators receive pool share by their share of engaged views among monetizing creators in that country, then keep 45%. — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- Views of "non-original Shorts (unedited clips, reuploads, compilations with no original content)", bot views, and views of content that breaks advertiser-friendly guidelines are excluded. — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)
- Only views after accepting the Shorts Monetization Module count. — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220?hl=en)

**India RPM: all unofficial estimates (anecdotal)**
- About ₹5–₹30 per 1,000 Shorts views for Indian audiences, versus ₹25–₹150 for long-form. — [upGrowth, Sep 2026](https://upgrowth.in/youtube-cpm-india-guide-2026/) (blog estimate)
- About ₹15–₹45 per 1,000 for mass entertainment, and ₹80–₹250 for finance and tech niches. — [newmoneymatrix](https://newmoneymatrix.org/youtube-shorts-rpm-india/) (blog estimate)
- Low-end estimate: 1M Shorts views in India ≈ ₹900–₹3,800 (global Shorts RPM ~$0.03–$0.08). — [schedpilot](https://schedpilot.com/how-much-youtube-pays-for-1-million-views-in-india/) (blog estimate)
- Regional-language Indian Shorts ≈ $0.01–$0.03 per 1,000 views. — [identitykit.in](https://www.identitykit.in/blog/youtube-shorts-monetization-india-2026) (blog estimate)
- "Some popular Indian Shorts creators report ₹20,000–₹1,00,000+ per 1M Shorts views when combined with sponsorships" (sponsorship-inclusive, not ad RPM). — [bosswallah](https://bosswallah.com/blog/creator-hub/1-million-youtube-shorts-views-india-income-monetization-guide-2026/) (anecdotal)

### Inferences
- At ₹5–₹30 RPM, the 10M-view YPP threshold would pay roughly ₹50,000–₹3,00,000 per 10M views. The lower end is more likely for Tamil, India-only audiences. This is arithmetic on blog estimates, not data.
- **Timing risk for this channel:** a channel starting in Nov 2026 is very unlikely to reach 10M Shorts views in 90 days before 1 Feb 2027. After that date it faces the 20M Shorts or 8,000-hour entry bar. Shorts ad revenue alone is a weak plan for a Tamil Shorts-only beginner. Fan funding, Shopping and brand deals are more reachable first.
- Avoiding licensed music keeps 100% of a Short's revenue in the pool, though the creator's own 45% allocation doesn't change. Self-made or royalty-free audio is neutral to slightly positive.

### Gaps
- The official 72851 page I fetched did not show the lower "fan funding / expanded YPP" tier's numbers. From training knowledge, not verified this session: 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, plus 3,000 watch hours in 12 months or 3M Shorts views in 90 days, available in India. Verify on the YouTube Help page before relying on it.
- No Tamil-specific RPM disclosures were found on Reddit (r/NewTubers, r/PartneredYoutube) or in Indian business press.

## (d) Rules on AI-generated, reused, "inauthentic" content and health misinformation

### Takeaway
AI tools are allowed. YouTube demonetizes **channels** (not just videos) for templated or mass-produced output, stitched shock AI clips, and AI personas posing as human experts on health, finance, legal or political topics. Disclosure is required only for **realistic** synthetic content. Fully animated or fantastical AI visuals and cloning your own voice do not need a label. Health content must not contradict health-authority guidance. Debunking is protected only when the video clearly disputes the myth, with context.

### Cited Findings
**Inauthentic content (monetization policy)**
- 15 July 2025: "repetitious content" was renamed "inauthentic content" to clarify it covers repetitive or mass-produced content. The reused content policy was unchanged. — [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Not allowed: content that "looks like it's made with a template"; templated storylines, image slideshows or scrolling text with little narrative or educational value; characters in the same situation with the same outcome; "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates" that looks mass-produced. — [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Allowed: the same intro and outro if the bulk differs; series where each video has a distinct concept; AI used to edit scripts, make a unique background visual, or visualize a character or narrative the creator invented. The final product "must still demonstrate your creative vision". — [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- 13 July 2026 restructure into three categories: (1) generic or repetitive; (2) **new label** "unsatisfying or off-putting", meaning emotionally manipulative formulas, stitched unrelated AI clips meant to shock, or deceptive realistic fakes such as a celebrity death or disaster; (3) **AI personas on sensitive topics**, where content that "presents itself as a human expert providing advice" on health, legal, finance or politics is not monetizable (e.g. an AI "doctor" promoting remedies). Tubefilter calls it clearer wording, not new rules. — [Tubefilter, 13 Jul 2026](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/); [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Enforcement context: in January 2026 YouTube removed a group of large AI "slop" channels. Reported figures (e.g. 11 terminated, 5 wiped) vary by outlet and come from blogs. — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-ai-slop-crackdown-2026/) (secondary, figures unverified)

**Reused content**
- Not allowed: repurposing others' content without significant commentary or modification; content that "exclusively features readings of material you did not create, such as website text". It applies "even if you have permission", and is separate from copyright. — [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- These policies apply to the whole channel. Violations in some videos can remove monetization for the entire channel. — [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)

**AI disclosure ("altered or synthetic content")**
- Disclosure is required for realistic content that makes a real person appear to say or do something they didn't, alters real events or places, generates a realistic scene that didn't happen, or AI music that is the main focus. — [YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)
- Not required: fantastical or fully animated content; filters and effects; production help (scripts, titles, thumbnails, captions); **cloning your own voice for voiceovers or dubs**. — [YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)
- Label placement: in the player for photorealistic AI or AI music; in the expanded description for non-photorealistic or animated AI. YouTube may auto-label via its own GenAI tools, C2PA metadata, or detection. — [YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)
- Disclosure itself does not limit audience or monetization. Persistent non-disclosure can bring forced labels, removal, or YPP suspension. — [YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)

**Medical misinformation**
- Prohibited: content that contradicts health-authority guidance on prevention, transmission or treatment, e.g. promoting MMS, black salve or turpentine, or saying approved cancer treatments never work. — [YouTube Help 13813322](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13813322?hl=en)
- EDSA exception: content that aims to "condemn, dispute, or satirize misinformation" may be allowed with context in the video, audio, title or description, e.g. "countervailing views from local health authorities or medical experts". "This is not a pass to promote misinformation." — [YouTube Help 13813322](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13813322?hl=en)
- Penalties: first violation is a warning, which expires 90 days after completing policy training. A repeat within the window is a strike. Repeated violations lead to termination. — [YouTube Help 13813322](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13813322?hl=en)
- Health source information panels and health shelves in India are only for **licensed** health professionals or organisations, vetted in about 1–2 months. — [YouTube Help 12796915](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12796915?hl=en-GB); [Google India blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/new-ways-for-registered-healthcare-professionals-in-india-to-reach-people-on-youtube/)

### Inferences
- For this channel: fully animated AI visuals plus the creator's own Tamil voice likely need **no** disclosure under current rules. Photorealistic AI scenes of real-looking events, such as a realistic disaster, should be disclosed.
- The biggest monetization risk is the "template" pattern: the same structure, AI style and music on every Short with only the fact swapped. Vary the formats, keep the creator's own narration and viewpoint, and avoid image-slideshow builds.
- For health-myth debunks: state the myth only to dispute it, say the correct position on screen or in voiceover early, name the authority (WHO, ICMR, MoHFW) in the video or description, and never present an AI character as a doctor. A non-licensed creator cannot get the health information panel.
- A Short that only reads a Wikipedia or website text verbatim falls under "readings of material you did not create". Rewriting the script in the creator's own words matters.

### Gaps
- No official list of what counts as "enough variation" across a channel; enforcement is case by case.
- No Tamil or India-specific enforcement data found.

## (e) Multi-language audio / dubbing for Shorts and reusing visuals for an English version

### Takeaway
Auto-dubbing has been open to all creators since February 2026. Third-party guides say multi-language audio tracks can be added to Shorts. Reusing your own visuals for an English version on another channel is not "reused content" under the policy, as long as you made the originals.

### Cited Findings
- Auto-dubbing rolled out globally to all creators on 4 Feb 2026 with no sign-up. Language coverage is reported as 20–29 languages depending on direction, and "Expressive Speech" is available in 8 languages. — [gHacks, 5 Feb 2026](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/02/05/youtube-makes-auto-dubbing-available-to-all-creators-worldwide/); [sync.so](https://sync.so/blog/youtube-auto-dubbing) (secondary)
- Guides cite YouTube Help as saying multi-language audio tracks can be added "to a video or a Short". Auto-dubs do not translate titles. — [videodubbing.com](https://videodubbing.com/blog/post/youtube-multi-language-audio-tracks-setup-guide-2026/) (secondary)
- Reused-content allowances include "content that primarily features the uploading creator" and footage with substantial original editing. The policy targets repurposing others' content. — [YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en)
- Cloning your own voice for dubs needs no AI disclosure. — [YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491?hl=en)

### Inferences
- Option A: one channel with Tamil as primary audio plus an English or Hindi track. Views pool on one video, which helps reach YPP. Option B: a separate English channel splits views across two channels, each needing its own threshold. For the 10M/20M Shorts bar, option A is likely better.
- Mass-uploading near-identical Shorts across many channels could look "mass-produced". Keep any separate English channel genuinely edited for that audience.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm from an official YouTube page whether Tamil is a supported auto-dub source or target language, or whether Shorts get auto-dubs the same way as long-form.

## (f) Other income: brand deals, affiliate, Instagram/Facebook

### Takeaway
In India, YouTube Shopping affiliate (500+ subscribers and YPP) and brand deals through YouTube's merged BrandConnect/Creator Partnerships are the more reachable routes. Meta pays little directly in India: Facebook monetization is invite-only, and Instagram's options are gifts, subscriptions and brand deals.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube Shopping affiliate in India: the bar was lowered to 500 subscribers (from 1,000) in March 2026 and requires YPP. Partner retailers include Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Purplle, Shopsy and Tira. Made-for-Kids and music channels are excluded. — [Google India blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/earn-earlier-expanding-youtube-shopping-to-creators-with-500-subscribers/); [YouTube Help 13376398](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid)
- Average affiliate payouts to Indian creators rose more than 160% year on year, and more than 50% of eligible Indian creators had enrolled as of May 2026. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/advertising/youtube-india-creator-payouts-soar-160-percent-with-expanded-retail-partner-ws-lo-111332.htm); [Google India blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/helping-indian-creators-build-thriving-businesses-on-youtube-shopping/)
- Amazon product tagging for Indian creators was reportedly announced on 23 Sep 2026. Only one source; unverified. — [kudozz.in](https://www.kudozz.in/blog/youtube-shopping-india-creators)
- BrandConnect merged with the Creator Partnerships hub on 24 Mar 2026 and is available in India. Creators set their own long-form and Shorts rates in the Earn tab. Sources conflict on the remaining eligibility bar. — [lilachbullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/youtube-brandconnect/); [creaticalc](https://creaticalc.com/blog/youtube-creator-partnerships) (secondary)
- India brand-deal rates (anecdotal, unsourced): about ₹2,000–₹8,000 for an integrated mention by a nano creator (1K–10K subscribers). — [souravjoshivlogs.in](https://souravjoshivlogs.in/brand-deals-for-youtubers-in-india/). - Instagram in India: Gifts from 500 followers (about $0.01 per Star), Subscriptions reportedly from 10K followers, affiliate tagging is back in India, and brand deals are the main income. — [Stan](https://stan.store/blog/how-to-monetize-instagram-2026/); [webhippo.in](https://webhippo.in/blog/instagram-monetization-india); [fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/instagram-reels-earnings-per-view-india-2026) (secondary)
- Facebook: content monetization is invite-only. In-stream ads, Ads on Reels and the Performance bonus ended 31 Aug 2025. One blog estimates Indian Facebook Reels pay ₹0.50–₹3 per 1,000 views (unofficial). — [clip-short.com](https://www.clip-short.com/guides/facebook-content-monetization); [fluxnote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/facebook-reels-monetization-india-2026)
- YouTube's 2027 plan adds milestone incentives for Shopping and brand deals for channels below the 10M Shorts threshold. Details are not yet published. — [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-partner-program-updates-2027-new-opportunities-earn/)

### Inferences
- A science-facts niche suits affiliate products such as science kits, books and gadgets on Flipkart, and edtech or gadget brand deals. Reaching 500 subscribers plus YPP is the gate for Shopping.
- Cross-posting the same Shorts to Instagram and Facebook Reels mainly builds audience and brand-deal leverage. Direct payouts there are negligible in India.

### Gaps

## (g) Posting frequency and timing

### Takeaway
YouTube says the system has no daily-upload threshold and doesn't optimize for time of day. Daily posting mainly helps by giving more seed tests and faster learning, not through any algorithmic reward.

### Cited Findings
- Sherman, 23 Aug 2023: "There is nothing in our systems that's like when you hit the five video or 10 short threshold per day". On time of day: "It's not something that we think about optimizing for." — [OutlierKit with timestamped links](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/); [Creator Insider video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n3jsYK_-aRU)
- Sherman: "If that didn't go how you wanted to go, learn from it and move forward and try again." — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-algorithm/)
- YPP may switch off monetization after 6+ months without uploads (an inactivity floor, not a frequency reward). — [YouTube Help 72851](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851?hl=en)

### Inferences
- One Short per day for 30 days is reasonable as an experiment budget of 30 seed tests. Quality and variety matter more than cadence, given the inauthentic-content rules against templated output.

### Gaps
- No controlled or official 2025–2026 data on cadence or timing effects for Shorts. Statements from 2023 are the latest staff quotes found.
