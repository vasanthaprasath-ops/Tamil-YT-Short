# Tamil short-form video landscape (YouTube Shorts / Reels), 2024–2026

Method note: most primary evidence below comes from live YouTube search results pulled on 2026-11-04 through the in-app browser. These were signed-out searches, probably with an India locale. Each search's top ~12–30 Shorts were read with their view counts. Channel subscriber counts come from YouTube channel-search results on the same day. Search URLs follow the pattern `https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=<term>`, and the term is given in each citation. Shorts results show title and views but not the channel, so a channel is named only when the title or hashtag names it. View counts are for individual Shorts that YouTube chose to surface. They are a sample, not an average. Instagram Reels were **not** checked (see Gaps). Per the coordinator's scope change, this covers ALL categories, with the myth and city findings kept.

## 1. Which Tamil facts / explainer / science channels are biggest, and what formats/titles do their top Shorts use?

### Takeaway
Tamil "facts" Shorts is a crowded, proven, high-ceiling category. Several channels have 0.4M–3.4M subscribers, and individual Shorts reach 1M–22M views. The winning format is a single striking visual "wonder", narrated in Tamil, with a shock emoji (😱🤯) and a Tamil-script hook question plus English hashtags. Almost no one does honest verdict-style science.

### Cited Findings
- Channel sizes (YouTube channel search, 2026-11-04):
  - Tamil Facts (@Tamilfacts_Yoga) has 3.39M subscribers. vidIQ separately reports 3.39M subs and ~447M total views as of 2026-10-29, plus a recent 28-day subscriber *loss* — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@tamilfacts_yoga/)
  - Official Facts (@OfficialFacts_channel) has 2.62M. Its Shorts use the hashtags #tamilfacts #officialfacts (e.g. "😱 99% பேருக்கு இது பற்றி தெரியாது | facts about human psychology", 276K views) — YouTube search "tamil facts shorts"
  - Tamil Wonders (@tamilwonders) has 1.34M. Examples: "மனித நேயத்தின் கை | Tamil Wonders" at 15M views, and "கீரை இலை மனித இதயம் experiment" at 952K — YouTube search "tamil facts shorts", "tamil science shorts why"
  - Mr. GK (@MrGKTamil) has 1.7M. Its long-form "நீங்கள் நம்பிக்கொண்டிருக்கும் பொய்கள் | Myths Debunked" has 107,728 views — YouTube search "myth or fact tamil"
  - Sri info (@Sriinfo1) has 837K. Examples: "C4 explosive (education video) // Sri info (Tamil)" at 2.7M, and "Sobek Egypt God" at 965K — YouTube searches
  - DK IN FACTS (@DKINFACTS01) has 827K. Its title style is plain English, e.g. "dead sea | #shorts #tamil #facts #dkinfacts" (105K) and "Swimming pool leak detection" (1.6M) — YouTube search "tamil facts shorts"
  - Chitti - Tamil (@Chitti_tamil) has 574K. It is a science/maths education channel, and its "தீக்குச்சி Magnet-ல ஒட்டுமா?🔥🧲 | Chitti's Experiment" got 798K — YouTube search "tamil science shorts why"
  - Fast Facts தமிழ் (@FastFactsTamil) has 427K. "வெத்தலை வாடாம இருக்க இந்த ஐடியா தெரியுமா?" got 1.8M — YouTube search "தெரியுமா shorts"
  - SJ Tamil (@sjtamilofficial) has 382K. It runs a fixed title template, "<Tamil hook> | SJ Tamil #sjtamil #facts #tamil #shorts". Examples: "பெரிய கப்பல்களை இப்படி தான் நிறுத்துகிறார்" (20M), "யானையின் உடலில் ஒட்டுண்ணி" (2.7M), "இந்தியா மாதிரி ஜப்பானில் ஏமாற்ற முடியாது" (1.9M) — YouTube searches
  - Alfriffy (@alfriffyofficial) has 234K. "முதலைகளை ஏன் வேட்டையாடுறாங்க?🐊🥶" got 2.2M
  - Suryan Explains (@SuryanExplains) has 62.1K. It runs a "Myth vs Fact in tamil" series that gets 3.7K–12K per Short
- Other top single Shorts: "அபார சக்தி கொண்ட Einstein மூளை 😱 | facts in minutes" (22M), "ஒரு உண்மை சம்பவம் | Tamil Facts _ Fact Force" (10M), "இதுலயும் Science-ஆ 😱 | Fact Force" (1.8M), "Mystery Temples | Fact Flow Tamil" (6.9M), "1000 year old ganesh idol tamil" (5.4M), "How deep is ocean? | @dr.ragu_podcast" (1.8M) — YouTube search "tamil facts shorts". The "Fact Force" channel could not be resolved: channel search returned an unrelated "Hello Hari" (7.69M).
- Long-form anchor: Madan Gowri (@madangowri) has 9.01M subscribers (channel search). It is a Tamil explainer channel covering science, history and current affairs, with ~3.0B total views per a third-party estimate. He has said Tamil drew an audience because "there were less creators in Tamil" — [TNM](https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/science-history-how-youtuber-madan-gowri-s-tamil-explainers-became-hit-95309); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/creators-story/creators-inc-breaking-language-barrier-south-india-youtuber-kokru-madan-gowri)
- "Tamil Guru / Naveen Bharathi" Shorts: "பாண்டிச்சேரி ஏன் தமிழ்நாட்டோட இல்ல தெரியுமா?" (3.5M) and "என்ன உறுப்பு அது?" (3.3M). The channel's subscriber count was not resolved.
- The "தெரியுமா" keyword is diluted by devotional/astrology content, e.g. "மாலை 6 மணிக்கு மேல் செய்யக் கூடாத விஷயங்கள் தெரியுமா?" (3.6K) and many temple "ரகசியம்" Shorts at 1K–6K — YouTube search "தெரியுமா shorts"

### Inferences
- Top facts channels win on **visual spectacle** (ships, animals, experiments, weird objects), often built on licensed or re-used footage, not on argument. A faceless animated channel cannot compete on spectacle footage. It has to compete on **story, character and a payoff/verdict**.
- The 😱/🤯 + Tamil question + English hashtag title template is the category norm. It is a baseline to meet, not a differentiator.

### Gaps
- No reliable Shorts-only ranking (Playboard/Social Blade Shorts views) was retrieved. Subscriber counts mix Shorts and long-form.
- Average views per Short for these channels are unknown. Only top-surfaced Shorts were seen.

## 2. Who already does Tamil myth-vs-science / "elders said" content, how well does it do, and is anyone honest?

### Takeaway
The "முன்னோர்கள் சொன்னது அறிவியல்" niche is almost entirely **pro-tradition justification** with tiny view counts (15–3K). Generic "Myth vs Fact" Tamil Shorts get 1K–200K. **Health** myth-busting by doctors is the one honest version that clearly works (1M–4.4M). No one found runs an animated, recurring "paati said X → verdict UNMAI/POI" format that sometimes says false.

### Cited Findings
- Search "முன்னோர்கள் சொன்னது அறிவியல் காரணம்": the top results are long-form and pro-tradition, e.g. "தமிழ் கலாச்சாரத்தின் அறிவியல் காரணம் தெரியுமா? ...மூடநம்பிக்கை அல்ல முற்றிலும் அறிவியல்" (304 views), "நமது முன்னோர்கள் ஒன்றும் முட்டாள்கள் இல்லை" (1,113), and "முன்னோர்கள் சொல்வதற்கு இது தான் காரணம்" (3,097). One Short shown as "முன்னோர்கள் கண்ட அறிவியல்" got ~7.3K — YouTube search, 2026-11-04
- "பாட்டி சொல்லும் கதை – Episode 2 | உண்மையா? பொய்யா?" got **63 views**. This is a near-identical concept that has not taken off — YouTube search "உண்மையா பொய்யா shorts"
- "உண்மையா பொய்யா" is dominated by astrology and spirituality, and those get real views: "ஜோசியம் ஜாதகம் உண்மையா? பொய்யா?" (176K), "ஜோதிடம் உண்மையா பொய்யா? கிருஷ்ணரே சொன்ன பதில்! | Madurai Ramakrishnan" (437K), "கண் திருஷ்டி உண்மையா?" (28K), "செய்வினை உண்மையா பொய்யா" comedy (159K) — same search
- Paati vaithiyam (grandma remedies) is a big **uncritical** niche. Remedy Shorts get 50K–250K, e.g. "பேதிக்கு வீட்டிலேயே இருக்கும் மருந்து! | Paattu Vaithiyam Tips" (250K) and "நீர் கோர்வை தலைவலிக்கு" (196K). None of those seen evaluate efficacy — YouTube search "paati vaithiyam science"
- Generic myth/fact Shorts: "Myth or Truth | Biscuitswithtea" is a quiz/game format (827K, 375K). "Ultimate Myth vs Fact Tamil" quiz got 195K. "❌ Myth vs ✅ Fact | Health Myth Tamil" got 25K. Suryan Explains got 3.7K–12K — YouTube search "myth or fact tamil"
- Honest health myth-busting: Doctor Karthikeyan (@drkarthik, 2.89M subs) posted "Myth vs Science! | Tamil Health" (1.11M views, long-form). Other Shorts: "zero sugar - எனும் ஏமாற்று வேலை!! | Neeya Naana | Dr Arunkumar" (4.4M), "Health Myths vs Facts You Shouldn't Ignore" (2.9M), "Are Airfryers safe? will they cause cancer?" (59K) — YouTube search "health myth tamil doctor shorts"
- A Tamil/English skeptic precedent exists outside Shorts: Science Is Dope (Pranav Radhakrishnan) debunks ayurveda, homeopathy and Vedic maths claims — [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/science-is-dope-pranav-radhakrishnan-youtube-sadhguru-baba-ramdev-homeopathy-ayurveda-vedic-maths-pseudoscience). Tamil YouTube also hosts pseudoscience and conspiracy figures such as Paari Saalan — [TNM](https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/bigg-boss-vaccines-skirts-tn-conspiracy-theorists-see-big-illuminati-hand-all-78691)

### Inferences
- Demand is proven for "is X true?" questions (astrology 176K–437K, health 1M+). Supply of **honest, entertaining, recurring** answers is near zero. The 1K–5K figures the coordinator saw reflect weak execution (long-form, static images, defensive tone), not lack of interest.
- Doctor-led health myth content proves audiences accept "this is false" verdicts on body/food topics. That makes those the safest POI targets. Astrology/drishti attracts views but carries the same backlash risk as god/temple content.
- The 63-view "பாட்டி சொல்லும் கதை – உண்மையா? பொய்யா?" shows that the concept alone is not enough. The hook, animation quality and verdict payoff carry it.

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether any Tamil Shorts channel publishes explicit "POI" verdicts against tradition at scale. None surfaced.
- Comment sentiment on debunking content (backlash vs approval) was not sampled.

## 3. City / local-identity content (Madurai, Chennai, Kovai, Trichy, Pondy, Tirunelveli)

### Takeaway
Local identity is one of the strongest Tamil Shorts levers, but it lives mostly in **slang comedy** (1M–7.7M) and **food** (10M+), not in facts. "Why is city X like this" explainers exist and can hit big (Pondicherry 3.5M–3.9M, "Tamil Nadu's capital before Chennai" 11M). Few run as a series.

### Cited Findings
- Slang comedy:
  - "Chennai Girl Vs Nellai Aunty😅" (7.7M), "Madurai 🔄 Coimbatore Slang" (3.4M), "coimbatore kongu slang" (3.1M)
  - "Kanniyakumari or Tirunelveli slang? | Wils Pat" (2.5M; Wils Pat has 3.23M subs), "Nellai vs Madurai Slang | Ramkumar Comic" (2.4M), "Tirunelveli Slang" (2.3M)
  - "COIMBATORE SLANG~~KUSUMBU!" (1.5M), "கோவை Slang in English" (654K), "Average Kongu people's Slang" (377K)
  - Sources: YouTube searches "coimbatore kongu slang shorts", "tirunelveli slang comedy shorts"
- Food: "Madurai Famous Foods🫣♨️" (10M), "Coin Parotta Sapputtu irrukkingala" (13M), "Man Paanai Dum Parotta Making" (12M), "Biriyani | the_chennai_explorer" (11M), "117 years oldest Mess hotel... Srivilliputhur" (2.9M), "Which Tamil Nadu Food Is Older?" (2.7M). Aarif's MindVoice (6.57M subs) had "Madurai food is vera level" at 795K — YouTube searches "madurai la mattum shorts", "tamil food history shorts"
- City explainers:
  - "Do You Know Which Is Tamil Nadu's Capital Before Chennai?" (11M), "Why Pondicherry Is Separate from Tamil Nadu?" (3.9M), "பாண்டிச்சேரி ஏன் தமிழ்நாட்டோட இல்ல தெரியுமா?" (3.5M)
  - "Will Chennai Sink Into the Sea?" (1.2M), "What If Tamil Nadu Became an Independent Country?" (3.3M), "Real Demonte Colony History" (748K), "Coimbatore mystery house!" (2.9M)
  - Weaker examples: "How Pondicherry became part of India | Dreamea Tamil" (466K; channel 130K subs) and "Why Does Chennai Flood So Quickly?" (195K)
  - Low-effort "Chennai Facts in Tamil" Shorts get ~1.1K
  - Sources: YouTube searches "chennai facts tamil shorts", "pondicherry facts tamil shorts", "what if tamil shorts"
- Literal "Madurai la mattum" phrasing: "Madurai la mattum leave illa" got 1.6K and "Pesama Madurailaiye irunthurukalam😭" got 586K. The phrase is used as a meme/vibe, not as an explainer series — YouTube search "madurai la mattum shorts"
- Trichy: the only strong hit seen was nostalgia, "90s Tamil Nostalgia ❤️ அப்பாவின் அன்பு! | 90s Trichy life" (2.44M) — YouTube search "90s kids tamil nostalgia shorts"

### Inferences
- The proven emotional driver is **inter-city rivalry and pride** (Chennai vs Nellai, Madurai vs Kovai) and "share with your X friends" tagging. A city series should borrow that ("Madurai vs Kovai: yaar correct?") and end on a factual explanation.
- "Why is X separate/different" geography/history questions are the explainer sweet spot (Pondy 3.5M–3.9M). Generic "facts about Chennai" lists flop.

### Gaps
- No Trichy- or Tirunelveli-specific explainer series was found. It is unclear whether that is a gap or a search artefact.
- Instagram Reels, where much slang comedy lives, was not checked.

## 4. All categories that work, ranked: evidence of performance vs. gap for a faceless animated channel (scope expansion)

### Takeaway
By raw views, the top categories are **school/family comedy, food, slang comedy, Tamil-kings history and spectacle facts** (top Shorts at 10M–64M). The best **gaps** for a faceless animated channel are: honest myth/health verdicts, human-body facts in animation, "what if" Tamil Nadu scenarios, animated Tamil history beyond hype edits, and animated nostalgia. Supply is thin or low-quality in each, and each has proven demand nearby.

### Inferences
- **Ranking by performance (top-surfaced ceiling):** comedy > food > history hype > spectacle facts ≈ life hacks > slang > horror > nostalgia ≈ body ≈ what-if > mystery > psychology ≈ money > myth/elders-said.
- **Ranking by opportunity for a faceless animated channel:**
  1. Honest myth/health verdicts ("Paati sonnadhu unmaiya?"). Demand is proven (health myths 1M–4.4M), honest animated supply is about zero, and the verdict creates comment debate.
  2. Human body / food-in-body animated explainers. Demand ceiling is 3.3M and supply is inconsistent. The HEART-vs-LIVER spread suggests the topic hook matters more than the channel.
  3. Tamil Nadu "what if" and "why is X like this" geography/history. There are 3.3M–11M one-off hits and no consistent series.
  4. Animated Tamil history as a story (not a "மிரளவைத்த" hype edit). The audience is huge (Chola/Pandya 10M–17M), and animation gives a quality edge over stock-clip edits. Accuracy and caste/pride sensitivities apply.
  5. Animated nostalgia (paati veedu, 90s school). Ghibli-style AI already gets 150K–175K, while live-action peaks at 2.4M. This is a good secondary series, not a core one.
  6. Survival ("what would you do if...") in Tamil. There is barely any native supply, but only one proven hit.
- Avoid as core: comedy/slang and food. They are face- and location-driven, so animation is a disadvantage, though city rivalry can be borrowed as a hook. Also avoid astrology/temple, which carries misinformation and backlash risk.

### Gaps
- Views are top-surfaced samples, so no median or retention data was available. Rankings are directional estimates.
- The money/finance category may be dominated by face-led finfluencers not caught by Shorts search.

## 5. What do Tamil audiences engage with most? Industry data on regional short video

### Takeaway
Industry data confirms regional languages are the majority of Indian digital video. **No Tamil-specific Shorts/Reels statistics** were found in YouTube India, Meta, FICCI-EY or Kalaari/Redseer coverage. YouTube executives name Tamil and Telugu as heavy-use cohorts.

### Cited Findings
- A YouTube APAC partnerships director described Telugu and Tamil as "heavy-use cohorts" and said Shorts is helping regional markets grow. The interview is undated in the results — [Storyboard18](https://stgcms.storyboard18.com/?p=2277)
- YouTube CEO Neal Mohan said Shorts, which launched first in India, has "trillions of views" there — [The Hans India](https://thehansindia.com/business/youtube-shorts-now-has-trillions-of-views-in-india-ceo-neal-mohan-897671)
- Google's 2025 India trends post says 77% of Indian Gen Z watched content translated from another language — [Google India blog](https://blog.google/intl/en-in/products/platforms/2025s-biggest-trends-on-youtube-that-gave-india-a-new-shared-vernacular/)
- FICCI-EY 2025 coverage gives 572M online video viewers. Regional languages are ~54–56% of OTT consumption, with sources disagreeing on the exact figure — [Open Magazine](https://openthemagazine.com/branding-marketing-and-advertising/digital-tightens-its-grip-on-indias-278-trillion-media-market); [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/media-tv-news/viewership-for-hindi-language-tv-content-dropped-to-below-50-in-2024-142267.html)
- An older, non-FICCI claim says Hindi plus regional languages were 95% of Indian short-video consumption by 2021 — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/brandsutra/500-million-reasons-to-go-regional-why-language-is-indias-biggest-digital-advantage) (label: 2021 data, indicative only)
- The YouTube India Impact Report 2025 frames regional creators as a growing share, per a secondary summary — [Ascendants](https://ascendants.in/industry_events/youtube-india-impact-report-2025-regional-creators-global-reach/)

### Inferences
- Engagement themes visible in the YouTube sample are school relatability, city rivalry, food pride, Tamil-king pride and 90s nostalgia. All of these are identity and belonging content. Facts content that ties back to "us" (Tamil Nadu, paati, our city) should outperform generic world trivia.

### Gaps
- No Tamil-specific Shorts/Reels share, watch-time or genre breakdown was found in YouTube Culture & Trends, Meta, KPMG, Redseer or Kalaari material. The original reports were not fetched.

## 6. Title language: Tamil script, Tanglish or English?

### Takeaway
The dominant pattern is **mixed**: a Tamil-script hook phrase, an emoji, a "| Channel" tag, then English hashtags (#tamil #facts #shorts). Comedy and slang creators use Tanglish (Roman script). Some 3M–11M hits use **English-only** titles about Tamil topics.

### Cited Findings
- Tamil-script hooks plus English hashtags: SJ Tamil (20M), Tamil Wonders (15M), and the history hype Shorts ("மிரளவிட்ட தமிழர்கள் 💥 மூவேந்தர்கள் டா 🔥 #shortsfeed #tamil", 17M) — YouTube searches above
- English-only titles on Tamil topics: "Do You Know Which Is Tamil Nadu's Capital Before Chennai?" (11M), "Why Pondicherry Is Separate from Tamil Nadu?" (3.9M), "What If Tamil Nadu Became an Independent Country?" (3.3M), "How Big Was the Chola Empire - Tamil" (1.8M)

## 7. Faceless / AI-animated Tamil channels and how they are received

### Takeaway
Tamil AI-animated Shorts are already common. They are mostly comedy (fruits/vegetables/animals), Ghibli-style nostalgia, and 3D "body facts". Results range widely (24 views to 7.9M). Platform-level scrutiny of "AI slop" rose in 2025, so originality and real information value matter for monetisation.
