Research date: 2026-11-04. "Fetched 2026-11-04" means the page was read live that day. Anything marked **[UNVERIFIED]** came from a single third-party source, a search snippet, or a paraphrase that was not checked against a primary source.

## Q1. Is CapCut available or legal in India in 2026, on desktop and mobile?

### Takeaway
No. CapCut is still blocked in India as of late October / early November 2026, on mobile and in practice on desktop, and no reinstatement has been announced. One correction to the brief: CapCut was **not** on the 29 June 2020 list of 59 apps. It was blocked about a month later, in late July 2020, in a follow-up round of roughly 47 "clone" apps. Indian creators mostly use Instagram Edits, Adobe Premiere mobile or KineMaster on phones, and Clipchamp or DaVinci Resolve on PC.

### Cited Findings
- Contemporaneous reporting (Inc42, dated 5 Aug 2020) says the government banned "nearly 47 mobile applications for cloning the 59 Chinese apps" in the week before, including "ByteDance-owned video editing app CapCut" and Xiaomi's Mi Browser Pro. The article names no legal provision. — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/?p=220316)
- OfficeChai dates that second round to about 24 July 2020. — [OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/stories/xiaomis-browser-app-bytedance-app-capcut-apps-banned-india-initial-ban-59-apps)

- Some 2026 SEO blogs claim CapCut was on the June 2020 list, or banned in 2023. Both claims conflict with the 2020 reporting above. — [videowizardtools](https://videowizardtools.com/why-is-capcut-banned-in-india/) (conflicting claim)
- Status snapshot from a page dated 3 Nov 2026: CapCut is "Not available through official Indian app stores (October 2026)". - Multiple 2026 guides agree that the app is blocked, that the Section 69A orders have not been withdrawn, and that modified APKs from unofficial sources get no official updates and can carry malware. These are SEO/vendor blogs; there is no official MeitY statement for 2026. — [comparecrest](https://comparecrest.com/capcut-alternatives-india/); [dicloak](https://dicloak.com/blog-detail/is-capcut-banned-in-india-in-2026-what-users-need-to-know); [smartpostly](https://www.smartpostly.com/blogs/why-is-capcut-banned-in-india-the-complete-story-behind-the-ban/)

### Inferences
- Treat CapCut as off the table on every surface: mobile, desktop and web. A VPN or sideloaded APK adds malware risk and a terms-of-service problem, and buys nothing that Edits or Premiere mobile don't already give for free.
- The government blocking order is the legal basis. Using CapCut is not criminalised for individuals in any source found, but no legitimate route to install or run it exists.

### Gaps
- No primary MeitY or PIB document for the July 2020 CapCut order, and no 2026 government statement, was found. Status rests on press reports and 2026 blogs.
- Whether the CapCut web editor loads at all from Indian IPs in November 2026 was not independently tested.

## Q3. How accurate is Tamil auto-captioning, and what is the workflow to an .srt and burned-in captions?

### Takeaway
YouTube's own auto-captions officially support Tamil, including on Shorts, and are editable in Studio. That is the cheapest path to a Tamil-script .srt. Off-the-shelf Whisper large-v3 is weak on Tamil (about 49% WER on FLEURS in one 2026 benchmark). AI4Bharat IndicConformer roughly halves that (about 25%), and Sarvam's Saaras v3 claims Tanglish-aware Tamil at about ₹30/audio-hour. Because Vasanth writes the script himself, the script text should be the caption text, with STT used only for timing.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube automatic captions support **Tamil** (also Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi), for long-form videos and Shorts. Captions are edited in Studio → Subtitles. YouTube warns they "might misrepresent the spoken content due to mispronunciations, accents, dialects, or background noise". (Fetched 2026-11-04.) — [YouTube Help 6373554](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6373554)
- FLEURS-Tamil test set (586 read-speech clips), same normaliser for all models, self-reported by Lemura Labs (2026):
  - Whisper-large-v3: **48.58% WER / 13.75% CER**.
  - AI4Bharat IndicConformer-600M: **25.49% WER / 9.33% CER**.
  - Their own Qwen3-based fine-tune: 25.27% / 7.95%.
  — [osmapi/tamil-asr-qwen3 model card](https://huggingface.co/osmapi/tamil-asr-qwen3)
- Deepgram argues that OpenAI's text normaliser (which strips combining marks) hides Whisper's true WER for South Asian languages, so headline Whisper Tamil numbers look better than reality. This is a vendor-authored argument. — [Deepgram post via Plushcap](https://www.plushcap.com/content/deepgram/blog/how-openai-s-text-normalization-hides-whisper-s-true-word-error-rate-for-south-asian-and-southeast-asian-languages)
- Fine-tuned Whisper-large-v3 Tamil checkpoints report WERs of about 10–40%, on different datasets, so the figures are not comparable. — [Logii33/whisper-large-v3-ta](https://huggingface.co/Logii33/whisper-large-v3-ta/blob/main/README.md); [KhushiDS/whisper-large-v3-Tamil](https://huggingface.co/KhushiDS/whisper-large-v3-Tamil)
- Sarvam:
  - Saarika v2.5 is now **legacy**; Sarvam recommends **Saaras v3** (`model="saaras:v3"`). — [Sarvam docs – Saarika](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/getting-started/models/saarika.md)
  - Pricing is reported as ₹30 per audio-hour (₹45 with diarisation). **[third-party; official rate card not fetched]** — [smallest.ai](https://smallest.ai/hi/blog/sarvam-ai-speech-to-text-pricing)
  - Saaras v3 reportedly scores about 19.3% WER on IndicVoices (all languages, vendor-reported, Feb 2026). No Tamil-only figure was found. — [voice.lapaas.com, 13 Feb 2026](https://voice.lapaas.com/?p=16647)
  - Sarvam's Tamil page markets Tanglish/colloquial handling without publishing numbers. — [Sarvam Tamil STT](https://www.sarvam.ai/apis/speech-to-text/tamil)
- Subtitle Edit (free, Windows) has **faster-whisper built in** for batch transcription and correction. Users report the default faster-whisper can be slow, and that an **initial prompt** "helps — a lot". — [GoTranscript on Subtitle Edit](https://gotranscript.com/public/subtitle-edits-new-faster-whisper-feature-speedy-and-accurate-transcriptions); [VideoHelp forum](https://forum.videohelp.com/showthread.php?p=2785269)

- DaVinci Resolve auto-subtitles are Studio-only, so the free version imports an .srt. Clipchamp exports an .srt. — [SkyScribe](https://www.sky-scribe.com/en/blog/davinci-resolve-auto-captions-version); [Microsoft Support](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/clipchamp/how-to-use-autocaptions-in-clipchamp)

### Inferences
- **Workflow A (zero cost, recommended):**
  1. Export a rough cut and upload it to YouTube as **unlisted**.
  2. Let Tamil auto-captions generate.
  3. In Studio → Subtitles, replace the text with the **script text** and fix timing.
  4. Download the .srt, import it into Resolve or Clipchamp, and style and burn it in.
  5. Upload the final master as the public Short.
- **Workflow B (offline):** Subtitle Edit → faster-whisper large-v3, language `ta`, with the script pasted as the initial prompt → correct against the script → .srt. Expect heavy correction: at about 49% WER, Whisper output is a timing scaffold, not usable text.
- **Workflow C (API, needs scripting):** Saaras v3 or IndicConformer give a better first draft, but for a 1-minute self-scripted voiceover the accuracy gain matters less than timing.
- **Tamil script vs Tanglish:** burned-in captions in Tamil script match the channel's Tamil identity. Tanglish captions are easier for second-generation diaspora and non-Tamil-reading viewers but look informal. Either way, the script is the source of truth, not STT output. The editorial choice is Vasanth's; no data on CTR or retention differences was found.

### Gaps
- No independent Tamil WER figure was found for YouTube auto-captions, Google Cloud STT (Chirp), Azure Speech (ta-IN) or Saaras v3.
- Downloading .srt from YouTube Studio is not covered on the fetched help page. It is known Studio behaviour but **[UNVERIFIED in this session]**.
- No measurements were found on conversational versus read speech for Tamil. The FLEURS numbers are read speech only, per the model card.

## Q4. Which Tamil fonts have free commercial licences, and which render well in editors?

### Takeaway
Use Google Fonts Tamil families: **Mukta Malar, Catamaran, Hind Madurai, Noto Sans Tamil, Baloo Thambi 2, Anek Tamil, Arima Madurai, Kavivanar** and (unconfirmed) Pavanam. Google Fonts are released under the SIL OFL 1.1 (a few under Apache 2), which permits free commercial use. For bold white-with-black-stroke Shorts captions, the heaviest weights of **Catamaran (9 weights), Mukta Malar, Baloo Thambi 2 or Noto Sans Tamil** are the practical picks.

### Cited Findings
- Most Google Fonts use the **SIL Open Font License v1.1**, some use Apache 2, and the Ubuntu fonts use the UFL. Each family folder contains its licence file. — [google/fonts GitHub](https://github.com/google/fonts)
- Noto Sans Tamil is an unmodulated sans serif with multiple weights and widths. Hind Madurai is a 5-weight Tamil+Latin family from the Indian Type Foundry. Catamaran is a "highly versatile, 9-weight" family whose heavy weights can look dense. Mukta Malar (Ek Type) is suited to bilingual Tamil–Latin use. Others: Noto Serif Tamil, Arima Madurai, Kavivanar, Baloo Thambi 2, Anek Tamil. — [fonttamil.com – 10 best free Tamil Google fonts](https://fonttamil.com/blog/10-best-free-tamil-google-fonts.html); [Adobe Fonts – Hind Madurai](https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/hind-madurai); [Adobe Fonts – Noto Sans Tamil](https://fonts.adobe.com/fonts/noto-sans-tamil)
- Legacy-encoded Tamil fonts (e.g. Baamini) are not Unicode and break in modern tools. — [Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/indesign-discussions/problem-with-tamil-fonts/m-p/13143988)
- In one app, switching to Noto Sans Tamil did not fix a word-initial ப/வ rendering bug. The bug was in the app's shaper, not the font. — [Anki forum](https://forums.ankiweb.net/t/tamil-text-in-editor-starting-with-and-not-displayed-correctly-linux-ubuntu/67975)

### Inferences
- Download from fonts.google.com (official) or the google/fonts GitHub. Install the static .ttf files for every weight on Windows, because some editors handle variable fonts poorly.
- OFL lets you use the fonts in videos, including monetised ones, with no attribution, but you may not sell the font files themselves. Check the OFL.txt in the family folder to confirm. **[OFL terms not fetched this session]**
- If Tamil renders wrong, a font change rarely fixes it. Change the text tool (e.g. Resolve basic Text or Subtitle track instead of Text+), or pre-render the captions as PNGs.

### Gaps
- Pavanam's presence on Google Fonts and its licence were not verified in this session.
- No editor-by-font compatibility test exists online. It must be tested hands-on.

## Q5. How do music and SFX licences work (YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay, Freesound, Epidemic Sound, Meta Sound Collection), and does "no music" boost Shorts revenue?

### Takeaway
Using no licensed music does **not** raise Vasanth's personal share: he keeps 45% of his allocation either way, and his allocation is based on his engaged views. Music only shrinks the overall Creator Pool. The safest sources for the verdict-stamp sting are self-made sounds, YouTube Audio Library SFX (Content-ID-safe on YouTube) and Freesound CC0, with off-YouTube terms checked per track. Avoid Meta Sound Collection for anything uploaded to YouTube. Pixabay music carries Content ID claim risk.

### Cited Findings
**Shorts revenue sharing**
- Shorts Feed ad revenue is pooled, and part of it pays music licensing:
  - No music: "all of the revenue associated with it goes into the Creator Pool".
  - One track: half goes to the pool.
  - Two tracks: one-third goes to the pool.
- "Your allocation from the Creator Pool is not affected by your use of a music track." Creators keep **45%** of their allocated revenue "regardless if music was used or not". Only music counts as third-party content.
- "Shorts over one minute in length that contain claimed content are blocked and not eligible for monetization."
- (Fetched 2026-11-04.) — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)
- The same fetch reported this line: "Starting February 1, 2027, creators need at least 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days to earn Shorts ad and Premium revenue". **[UNVERIFIED: surfaced by the fetch tool's summary, not quoted verbatim; re-read the page before relying on it — it would be a major eligibility change]** — [YouTube Help 12504220](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)

**YouTube Audio Library**
- You "can monetize videos with music and sound effects from the Audio Library". Downloaded tracks "won't be claimed by a rights holder" through Content ID. Creative Commons tracks need the attribution text in the description. Use the "Attribution not required" filter to avoid that. "Only music and sound effects from the Audio Library are known to YouTube to be copyright-safe." The page does **not** address Shorts specifically or use outside YouTube. (Fetched 2026-11-04.) — [YouTube Help 3376882](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882?hl=en)
- Third-party guides say off-YouTube use (Instagram/Facebook) depends on each track's licence. CC-BY tracks are generally usable with attribution; YouTube-licence tracks may not be cleared outside YouTube; Instagram's own rights system may still mute audio. — [usethirdchair](https://usethirdchair.com/blog/can-you-use-youtube-audio-library-music-on-instagram); [Lickd](https://lickd.co/youtube-audio-library/)

**Pixabay**
- Free to use, no attribution required, may be modified. You may not redistribute content "on a Standalone basis", may not use it in misleading ways, and must check third-party rights yourself. The full licence at /service/terms is the binding text. — [Pixabay licence summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/)
- Some Pixabay composers register their tracks with Content ID, so claims happen. Pixabay has no Content ID whitelist; the remedy is to dispute with the Pixabay licence certificate or contact the composer. — [Thematic vs Pixabay](https://hellothematic.com/thematic-vs-pixabay/); [2022 blog on clearing claims](https://smarthustlers101.blogspot.com/2022/09/how-to-clear-youtube-content-id-claim.html)

**Freesound**
- Licences are CC0 (do "pretty much what you want"), CC BY (commercial use OK with credit) and CC BY-NC (no commercial use, so monetised videos are out). Sampling+ is a legacy licence being phased out and roughly follows BY-NC rules. Raw, unedited sounds can trigger Content ID. (Fetched 2026-11-04.) — [Freesound FAQ](https://freesound.org/help/faq/)

**Epidemic Sound**
- Creator plan: US$9.99/month billed annually, or US$17.99 month-to-month. Pro: US$16.99 annual / US$39.99 monthly (guide updated July 2026). — [Photutorial](https://photutorial.com/epidemic-sound-pricing)

**Meta Sound Collection**
- Usable commercially, but only on Meta products. Third-party sources say it cannot be used on YouTube. A 2017 copy of the terms reserves infringement claims for use outside the permitted uses. Current official terms were not fetched. — [allfacebook.de (2017 terms copy)](https://allfacebook.de/?p=49942); [Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/facebook-sound-collection/); [Cloud Cover Music](https://cloudcovermusic.com/music-licensing-guide/facebook-sound-collection)

### Inferences
- The "no licensed music" rule is right for cross-posting simplicity and Content ID safety, but its revenue effect is collective (a bigger pool), not a personal multiplier. The 45% share and the views-based allocation are unchanged.
- For the verdict-stamp sting, one master file is uploaded to all three platforms. The SFX must therefore be cleared everywhere: CC0 (Freesound), self-recorded or self-synthesised, or Adobe Premiere's Firefly "Generate Sound Effects" (terms not checked). YouTube Audio Library SFX are only guaranteed Content-ID-safe on YouTube.
- Keep a per-asset licence log (source URL, licence, download date) so any claim can be disputed quickly.

## Q6. What are the free audio clean-up options?

## Q7. What are the 2026 upload specs and safe zones for Reels and Shorts?

### Takeaway
The 1080×1920 (9:16) master works on all three platforms. Neither YouTube nor Meta publishes official **organic** safe zones; all pixel figures are third-party measurements and disagree. Vasanth's rule of keeping the bottom 20% clear (384 px) is slightly less than some Reels measurements (310–450 px bottom), so a bottom clearance of about 24% (≈450 px) plus about 220 px at the top is the conservative cross-platform choice.

### Cited Findings
- TikTok, Reels and Shorts all take the same 1080×1920 9:16 master. — [Frameo](https://frameo.ai/blog/reel-size/); [ClipSpeed](https://www.clipspeed.ai/tools/social-media-video-dimensions.html)
- Instagram Reels safe zone, two conflicting measurements:
  - Centre 1080×1440 (clear 220 px top, 450 px bottom). — [AdCreate](https://adcreate.com/blog/instagram-reel-dimensions-size-guide-2026)
  - 996×1400 (210 px top, 310 px bottom blocked). — [Upload-Post safe-zone checker](https://www.upload-post.com/tools/safe-zone-checker/)
- YouTube Shorts safe zone, three conflicting measurements:
  - Centre 984×1500. — [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/sizes/youtube/shorts)
  - About 210 px top and 270 px bottom. — [Wild and Free Tools](https://wildandfreetools.com/blog/vertical-video-safe-zones-guide-2026/)
  - 300 px bottom (channel name, subscribe, audio, description). — [AdMake](https://www.admakeai.com/blog/vertical-video-dimensions-2026)
- Cross-platform "guaranteed visible" zone: a centred 900×1400. Figures were reverse-engineered from in-app overlays; there are no official organic numbers. — [AdaptlyPost 2026 guide](https://adaptlypost.com/blog/social-media-safe-zones-2026-complete-guide)

### Inferences
- Safe caption band on 1080×1920: keep captions between about y = 1150 and y = 1450 px, and within about x = 90–918 px. That clears the right-side action rail (Vasanth's 15% = 162 px) and the bottom caption/handle area on all three apps.
- Keep the verdict stamp in the upper-centre third. Avoid the top ~220 px.
- Before publishing, preview each upload in the app's own draft view, since the overlays change with app updates.

### Gaps
- No official 2026 Meta or YouTube page on organic Reels/Shorts safe zones was found.
- Instagram/Facebook upload limits for 2026 (max length, bitrate, file size, 3-minute Reels) were not verified from Meta Help this session.
